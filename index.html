HOW TO PLAY

Launch our chill capybara as far as possible across the wild wetlands!

🎯
Aim & Power: Aim cannon with ↑/↓ arrows or drag. Press & hold SPACE or Launch Button to meter power, release to fire!
🍊
Zen Boosts: Press SPACE or tap the screen up to 3 times mid-flight for emergency upward speed!
💥
TNT Barrels: Mega explosive blast propelling you forward & high!
🍄
Trampolines: Bouncy launch pads!
♨️
Hot Springs: Soothing mud & hot water slides that boost speed!
🌵
Cacti & Mud: Sticky hazards that slow you down to a crawl!
